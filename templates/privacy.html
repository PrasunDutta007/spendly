Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what information the service collects, why it collects it, and what happens to it once it is stored.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the service needs in order to work:

  • Account details — the name and email address you provide when registering, along with a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself.
  • Basic technical data — information your browser sends with each request, such as the page requested and the time of the request, kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not connect to your bank, and we do not ask for card numbers or account numbers. Every financial figure in Spendly is one you typed in yourself.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service to you — and for nothing else. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Sign you in and keep your session active.
  • Display your expenses, monthly summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Filter and total your records over the date ranges you choose.
  • Diagnose errors and protect accounts from unauthorised access.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your expense records for marketing.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in the application's database. Passwords are hashed before storage, and access to the underlying systems is limited to what is required to operate and maintain the service.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your records from the active service. Please note that no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of outside services to run:

  • Hosting infrastructure — the provider that runs the application and its database.
  • Web fonts — typefaces loaded from a font provider, which means your browser contacts that provider when a page loads.

These providers process data only to deliver their part of the service, and your expense records are never passed to them for their own purposes. Any external site you reach from Spendly is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want a copy of your data, or would like your account deleted, get in touch and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change, and significant changes may also be communicated within the application.

You can also review our Terms and Conditions.
